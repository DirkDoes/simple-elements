# Timeline examples

Open **Patterns → Timelines** in the showcase for four editable examples: a simple scrubber, layers and chapters, an annotated recording, and highlighted intervals with disjoint watched segments.

Compose [Timeline](timeline.md), [Timeline Range](timeline-range.md), and [Timeline Marker](timeline-marker.md). Layout and color meanings belong to the application. The examples use local sample timestamps and do not load a video or enforce viewing permissions.
